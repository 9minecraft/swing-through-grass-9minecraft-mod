# Swing Through Grass — Fight Mobs Without Breaking Tall Grass

Swing Through Grass 9minecraft Mod lets you hit enemies and mine blocks that hide behind tall plants. Normally, when you swing a sword at a zombie standing in a field, you just chop down the weeds instead of hurting the monster. This mod fixes that problem by letting your attacks pass straight through flowers, ferns, and vines. You can finally fight in thick forests or grassy plains without clearing out the greenery first.

Your weapon swings pass straight through non-solid plants.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **exidex**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
